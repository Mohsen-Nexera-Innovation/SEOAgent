# SEO Agent Workflow

## 01. Define Website Goals

- Decide if the goal is direct sales, distribution, or lead generation.
- Based on the goal, determine the keyword type (Commercial / Informational / Navigational).

---

## 02. Collect Initial Keyword List

- Write down the products and services you offer (e.g., dental implants, surgical tools, clinic supplies).
- Extract initial keywords from product descriptions, competitor sites, or related content.

---

## 03. Use Keyword Research Tools

- Rely on tools such as **Google Keyword Planner**, **SEMrush**, or **Ahrefs**.
- Collect data for each keyword:
  - **Volume** (monthly search volume)
  - **CPC** (cost per click)
  - **KD** (keyword difficulty)

---

## 04. Competitor Analysis

- Search your main keywords in Google SERP and identify top-ranking sites.
- Determine direct competitors (same products/services) and indirect competitors (same audience).
- Gather the keywords they are targeting.

---

## 05. Classify Keywords

- Group keywords into categories:
  - **Commercial** (buy, price, order, supplier)
  - **Informational** (how, guide, benefits, comparison)
  - **Technical** (specific product names, medical terms)
- This helps map keywords to different page types.

---

## 06. Build the Final Keyword Sheet

- Create an Excel/CSV file with the following columns:
  - Keyword
  - Volume
  - CPC
  - KD
  - Type (Commercial / Informational / Technical)
  - Target Page

---

## 07. Map Keywords to Content

- Assign keywords to specific pages or products.
- Use them in:
  - **Title Tags**
  - **Meta Descriptions**
  - **H1/H2 Headers**
  - **Paragraphs and Body Content**
- Goal: automate On-Page SEO optimization.
